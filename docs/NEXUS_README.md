Clans

Guilds for Valheim, inspired by the guilds of Guild Wars. A clan has its own emblem to fly on banners, ranks its leader shapes, a shared treasury and storage, weekly clan activities and a clan level that unlocks upgrades.

Around that, Clans adds what several large groups need to share one server, whether they play together or against each other: parties and raids, shared skill experience, clan wars, chat channels, clan-only map sharing and tombstones that only your clan can open.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

Install with a mod manager, or put [font=Courier New]Clans.dll[/font] in [font=Courier New]BepInEx/plugins[/font] alongside Jotunn, YamlDotNet and Item Favorite Framework. Clans must be installed on the server and on every client.


[size=5][b]The Clans window[/b][/size]

Open it with the [b]Clan[/b] button in the row of round buttons at the top right of the inventory. The vanilla PvP toggle moves into the window's header. If another mod replaces the inventory screen (such as Auga), set a hotkey for the window in the client settings instead.

[list]
[*][b]Without a clan[/b], the window lists the server's clans so you can apply to one, or found your own for a fee.[/*]
[*][b]In a clan[/b], it has the Overview, Level, Members, War, Activities, Storage and Party tabs.[/*]
[/list]

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]Ranks and members[/b][/size]

A clan starts with five ranks. The leader can rename, reorder, add and remove ranks, and choose what each one may do: invite, kick, promote, manage wars, use storage, take from the treasury and more. Membership is per account, so all of a player's characters belong to the same clan.

[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/ClanOnline.png?raw=true[/img]

[size=4][b]Emblems[/b][/size]

Pick a background shape in a colour, then a symbol on top: a rune in a second colour, or a creature trophy. The emblem shows above your clanmates' heads and on your clan's banners.

[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/EmblemEditor.png?raw=true[/img]

[size=4][b]Treasury and storage[/b][/size]

Members donate coins to the treasury, which pays for storage tabs, clan levels and guild feasts. Shared storage tabs are bought from the treasury, and a later upgrade gives every member a personal tab too. Storage opens from anywhere and goes wherever its clan goes, so the server decides which items may be stored.

[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/ClanStorage.png?raw=true[/img]

[size=4][b]Wars[/b][/size]

One clan declares war, optionally with a kill target, and the other accepts. While at war, the two clans' members can fight each other whatever their PvP setting. A war ends in peace, when its time runs out, or when one side reaches the kill target.

[b]Declaring war[/b]
[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/War.png?raw=true[/img]
[b]At war[/b]
[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/War2.png?raw=true[/img]

[size=4][b]Tombstone lock[/b][/size]

Only a tombstone's owner and their clanmates can open it, so nobody can loot a player killed in PvP. Owners can also make theirs private.

[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/War3.png?raw=true[/img]

[size=4][b]Weekly activities[/b][/size]

Each clan can run each activity once a week, on Easy, Normal or Hard, for clan experience, treasury coins and rewards for everyone who took part.

[list]
[*][b]Trophy Hunt:[/b] hunt the creatures your clan is given, bosses included. Every member's kills count.[/*]
[*][b]Race:[/b] carry chests from a start totem to a destination far away. Carts and ships may carry them, portals may not.[/*]
[*][b]World Boss Battles:[/b] hold a totem against waves from the portals around it, then defeat a much stronger boss.[/*]
[/list]

Party and raid members roll Need, Greed or Pass for an activity's magic item rewards (with Item Favorite Framework and Epic Loot). The activity tracker under the minimap shows how your clan's activities are going.

[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/ClanActivity.png?raw=true[/img]
[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/ActivityTracker.png?raw=true[/img]

[size=4][b]Chat channels[/b][/size]

Global ([font=Courier New]/g[/font]), Clan ([font=Courier New]/c[/font]), Party ([font=Courier New]/p[/font]) and Raid ([font=Courier New]/r[/font]) channels, each with its own tab, and item links (Shift + right-click an item). The clan's status message greets members in chat when they log in.

[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/Chat.png?raw=true[/img]

[size=4][b]Movable HUD[/b][/size]

Drag the party frame, the activity tracker and the chat panel wherever you like, with the Layout button in the window header.

[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/LayoutAdjust.png?raw=true[/img]

[size=4][b]Also[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Clan map.[/b] Send map pings to your clan only, show your position to your clan only, and share your Epic Loot bounties with clanmates, each with a switch on the large map.[/*]
[*][b]Clan log.[/b] Every important action shows in the Overview tab's recent activity.[/*]
[/list]


[size=5][b]Clan levels[/b][/size]

Clans earn experience from activities and wars, but never level up by themselves. Once a clan has the experience, its members hand in the resources from the Level tab, and a rank allowed to upgrade the clan pays the coins from the treasury.

[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/ClanLevel.png?raw=true[/img]

What each level gives and costs, by default. The XP share is the part of each skill gain that clanmates in the same party receive.

[list]
[*][b]Level 1[/b] - 0 clan XP, 10 members, 25% XP share within 50 m. Unlocks the 1st storage tab and the Meadows feast.[/*]
[*][b]Level 2[/b] - 1,000 clan XP, 12 members, 27% XP share within 55 m. Unlocks the 2nd storage tab and the Black Forest feast. Costs 250 coins, 200 Wood, 150 Stone, 50 Resin, 20 Deer hide.[/*]
[*][b]Level 3[/b] - 2,500 clan XP, 15 members, 30% XP share within 60 m. Unlocks the personal storage tab and the Swamp and Ocean feasts. Costs 500 coins, 150 Fine wood, 100 Core wood, 30 Bronze, 10 Troll hide.[/*]
[*][b]Level 4[/b] - 5,000 clan XP, 18 members, 32% XP share within 65 m. Unlocks the 3rd storage tab and the Mountain feast. Costs 1,000 coins, 100 Ancient bark, 50 Iron, 20 Guck, 20 Withered bone.[/*]
[*][b]Level 5[/b] - 8,500 clan XP, 20 members, 35% XP share at any distance. Unlocks the Plains feast. Costs 1,500 coins, 50 Silver, 30 Wolf pelt, 50 Obsidian, 20 Crystal.[/*]
[*][b]Level 6[/b] - 13,000 clan XP, 25 members, 37% XP share at any distance. Unlocks the 4th storage tab and the Mistlands feast. Costs 2,500 coins, 50 Black metal, 50 Linen thread, 30 Tar, 15 Lox pelt.[/*]
[*][b]Level 7[/b] - 19,000 clan XP, 30 members, 40% XP share at any distance. Costs 3,500 coins, 100 Yggdrasil wood, 30 Refined eitr, 40 Carapace, 10 Black core.[/*]
[*][b]Level 8[/b] - 26,000 clan XP, 35 members, 42% XP share at any distance. Unlocks the 5th storage tab and the Ashlands feast. Costs 5,000 coins, 100 Blackwood, 30 Flametal, 30 Asksvin hide, 40 Ceramic plate.[/*]
[*][b]Level 9[/b] - 35,000 clan XP, 40 members, 45% XP share at any distance. Costs 7,500 coins, 60 Flametal, 50 Charred bone, 10 Morgen sinew, 60 Refined eitr.[/*]
[*][b]Level 10[/b] - 50,000 clan XP, 50 members, 50% XP share at any distance. Unlocks the Deep North feast. Costs 10,000 coins, 100 Flametal, 20 Black core, 5 Morgen heart, 80 Ceramic plate.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Guild feasts[/b][/size]

A member sets out a guild feast from the Level tab, right in front of them. It is a stronger copy of a vanilla biome feast that anyone may eat from, clan or not, as often as the game allows, until it is cleared away after half an hour. Each costs its biome's cooked meats and crops plus coins, paid by the member, or by the clan's treasury and shared storage when their rank allows it.

Health / stamina / eitr each feast gives, and the clan level it needs:

[*][b]Meadows[/b] (level 1) - 40 / 40 / 5[/*]
[*][b]Black Forest[/b] (level 2) - 40 / 40 / 10[/*]
[*][b]Swamp[/b] (level 3) - 40 / 40 / 15[/*]
[*][b]Ocean[/b] (level 3) - 50 / 50 / 20[/*]
[*][b]Mountain[/b] (level 4) - 50 / 50 / 25[/*]
[*][b]Plains[/b] (level 5) - 60 / 60 / 30[/*]
[*][b]Mistlands[/b] (level 6) - 70 / 70 / 38[/*]
[*][b]Ashlands[/b] (level 8) - 80 / 80 / 43[/*]
[*][b]Deep North[/b] (level 10) - 90 / 90 / 48[/*]
[/list]


[size=5][b]Clan banners[/b][/size]

Clan members can build clan banners in seven colours: white, black, blue, red, green, yellow and orange. Each is built like the vanilla banner of its colour, from the same recipe, and shows the builder's clan emblem to everyone. Only clan members can build them.

[img]https://github.com/MidnightsFX/Valheim_Clans/blob/master/docs/Banners.png?raw=true[/img]


[size=5][b]Configuration files[/b][/size]

The YAML files live in [font=Courier New]BepInEx/config/Clans/[/font]. The server's copies are sent to every player, changes are picked up as soon as a file is saved, and server admins can also edit them in game from the Mod Config menu. Each file explains its own settings at the top.

[list]
[*][b][font=Courier New]ranks.yaml[/font][/b] - The ranks and permissions a newly founded clan starts with.[/*]
[*][b][font=Courier New]levels.yaml[/font][/b] - Each clan level: the experience needed, member limit, upgrade cost and clanmate XP share.[/*]
[*][b][font=Courier New]emblems.yaml[/font][/b] - The emblem backgrounds, symbols and colours players can pick from.[/*]
[*][b][font=Courier New]storage.yaml[/font][/b] - The price and clan level of each storage tab, and which items may be stored.[/*]
[*][b][font=Courier New]activities.yaml[/font][/b] - The weekly activities: difficulty, targets, bosses and rewards.[/*]
[*][b][font=Courier New]feasts.yaml[/font][/b] - The guild feasts offered: the clan level each needs, its food and its cost.[/*]
[/list]
